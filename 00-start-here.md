## The business in 5 lines
1. LuciPet sells pet supplements for eye care and for skin and coat care.
2. Customers are pet owners, mostly women aged 20 to 50s.
3. All sales are through the website, lucipet.shop.
4. Based in Seoul, Korea. Trading for 0 to 2 years.
5. What makes us different: easy feeding, high palatability, premium quality, made in Korea.

## My guardrails
- Never send an email, message or post without my yes.
- Never spend money, place an order or give a refund without my yes.
- Never change prices or discounts without my yes.
- Never delete anything. Archive it instead.
- Always tell me where a number came from, and the date.
- Ask me before contacting a customer or supplier.
- Never make up a number. If you don't know, say so.
- Never share customer details outside the business.

## House rules
1. Open one file at a time. Never open a folder to see what is in it; the index says.
2. Numbers that change (sales, stock, cash) come from a connected tool. Name the tool and the date.
3. If the answer is not in the brain or a tool, say so. Never guess.
4. Add to the existing file. Never start a second file on the same topic.
5. Draft, don't do: nothing is sent, paid, posted or deleted without the owner's yes.
6. A new rule or decision goes in that area's "rules and decisions" file with today's date.
7. The brain is text only. Never put files (photos, invoices, PDFs) in this repository.
8. Save at the end of each conversation, in one commit with a short message saying what changed. Decisions and new rules are saved straight away.
9. This repository is the business's memory. Notes that don't have a home yet go in inbox.md; they're filed during the weekly tidy. Never save passwords, card or bank details, or customer personal details.
